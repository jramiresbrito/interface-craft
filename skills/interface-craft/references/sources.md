# Sources

Each rule in this skill comes from one of these. Read the originals; they are better than any summary.

## People and guides

- **Emil Kowalski** – design engineer, author of Sonner and Vaul, teacher of animations.dev. Skills (MIT): https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills – especially `emil-design-eng`, `animation-vocabulary`, `break-ui`, `mobile-native`. Site: https://emilkowal.ski/skill
- **Vercel Web Interface Guidelines** (MIT): https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines
- **Rauno Freiberg** – design engineer at Vercel, author of cmdk and Devouring Details. "Invisible Details of Interaction Design": https://rauno.me/craft/interaction-design
- **Refactoring UI** – Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger, https://www.refactoringui.com (book; rules restated in our words)
- **visual-explainer** – a Claude Code skill by nicobailon for diagrams and explainer pages.
